body:
  - At a previous job, preparing the night audit took far longer than it should have — most of the time went into tracking down information about each building. That's where this project came from. I built an interface that keeps all the necessary information in one place and gives management a clear overview of the status of every building.
  - Building this required setting up permissions to pull data from the database and reflect it in the interface in real time. It was also where I got to apply what I'd studied in my data science major — collecting the data, running the calculations, and presenting the numbers in a form management could actually use.
  - The overview leads with today — revenue, total pax, capacity, vacant beds — then splits into arrivals and departures expected versus actually checked in and out, with out-of-order rooms called out in red so nothing gets sold that cannot be used.
  - Every table exports, every view carries a date, and the night audit tracks room assignments live so the handover is a screen rather than a stack of notes.
