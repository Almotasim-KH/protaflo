  - 'Most budget apps tell you how much you spent. Few tell you whether that amount was reasonable. Budgettr starts from the one number that answers it: income minus expenses this month. The analytics, the bills, and the savings goals come after it, to explain where it came from.'
  - I designed and built it myself. The interface works in both languages, and amounts are in SAR on every screen. There is a dark theme. Every category gets a bar that shifts colour as it fills, so you see the problem before it becomes one. Bills carry their state — due or overdue — so you never find out about a delay after the time to act has passed.
