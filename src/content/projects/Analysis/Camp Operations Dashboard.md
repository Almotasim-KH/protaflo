  - "1. Context: Extension of an earlier dashboard; after review, the team asked for deeper insight and more detail."
  - "2. Problem: Correct numbers were hard to pin down. Data was scattered across 12 separate Excel sheets, not analysis-ready, and the team spent roughly 9 hours a week piecing manual reports together from them."
  - "3. Approach: I gathered all 12 sheets, cleaned and organized the data with Python into one final dataset, then designed a Power BI dashboard covering revenue, beds, persons and category averages across all 4 quarters. I could have just tidied the 12 sheets in place, but that still means switching between files every morning, so I replaced them with one merged dashboard instead."
  - "4. Result: Twelve spreadsheets replaced by one screen, roughly 9 hours saved weekly on manual reporting, and the team could spot categories needing further attention."
  - "5. Lesson: A dashboard that has to be opened fresh every morning needs to be designed for that habit, not just for the one report it started as."
