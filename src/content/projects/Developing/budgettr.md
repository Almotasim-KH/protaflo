body:
  - A platform that lets you manage your expenses and organise your income in a simpler way.
  - At the beginning of each month, you enter your monthly income and define the categories or areas where you usually spend your money.
  - You can then easily record your expenses by entering the amount and selecting the relevant category. You can also set a financial goal, and as you add your expenses and data, your progress is automatically updated and your completion percentage increases accordingly.
  - Once everything is set up, you’ll have a complete Dashboard that gives you a clear overview of your financial situation. It shows where your money is going, how much you’ve spent, how much you have left, and how you’re progressing toward your financial goal.
  - I designed this platform based on a problem I personally faced, with the goal of creating a solution that is simple, intuitive, and easy to use.
  - The interface is available in both Arabic and English, with all amounts displayed in Saudi Riyals (SAR) across the platform. It also includes Dark Mode and Light Mode, along with many additional features and details that you can discover for yourself while exploring the platform.
