body:
  - A website that allows stores to showcase their products and attract new customers. The site is managed through an admin panel that handles inventory and provides sales insights and revenue reports. It supports both Arabic and English.. 
  - This project was a real challenge. Its where I started learning about APIs, integrations, payment gateways, and delivery APIs — all the connections a website needs in order to run.
  - The admin side show how the business actually thinks. Sales over the last 14 and 30 days, live order feed with per-order status, product and stock management with low-stock alerts, a customer list, and branch records with opening hours and a map pin for each location.
